How it works

Scan, classify, remediate with approval, report

  1. Scan

    Read-only agents inspect the datasets you scope, inside your own project and EU region.

  2. Classify

    Findings are classified by data category and risk, with a deterministic, explainable score.

  3. Remediate with approval

    Agents propose masking, retention or documentation fixes. Your team approves or rejects each one and runs the approved changes.

  4. Report

    You get a clear report and an audit log of every decision, ready for your DPO, CISO or auditor.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What happens in the free 20-minute AI Act data scan?

A short video call. We go through where your company uses or plans to use AI, which data it touches, and which AI Act and GDPR questions are most likely to apply. You get a short written list of next steps. No data access is needed for this call.

Does our data leave our environment?

Scans are designed to run inside your own Google Cloud project, in EU regions. Detection runs on your side; the AI models receive metadata such as column names, categories and counts, not raw personal data.

Do the agents change anything automatically?

No. Agents propose findings and remediation scripts. A person on your team approves or rejects each one, and your team executes approved changes. Every decision is logged.

Are you certified or a law firm?

No. Cooploy is a technical consultancy, not a law firm or certification body. We provide technical evidence and remediation support that your legal, privacy and security teams can use.

Which companies do you work with?

Dutch mid-market companies running, or moving to, Google Cloud. We do not work with banks, insurers or other financial-sector organisations.

Do you only work on Google Cloud?

Our tooling is built for Google Cloud (BigQuery, Sensitive Data Protection, Dataplex, dbt). If your data lives elsewhere, the scan call is still useful and we will tell you honestly whether we are a good fit.
